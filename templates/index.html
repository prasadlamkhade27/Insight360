  3. Add Milestones: Set profit targets at different account balance levels.
  4. Calculate: Click "Calculate Strategy" to see projections.

Understanding the Parameters

💰 Starting Balance

Your initial trading capital. This is the amount you're starting with.

📈 Daily Return Target

The percentage profit you aim to make per day. Be realistic - 1-3% is considered healthy.

🎯 Win Rate

The percentage of trades you expect to win. Even 55% is good for profitable trading.

📊 Risk-to-Reward Ratio

How much you risk vs. how much you stand to gain. 1:1.5 means you risk $1 to make $1.50.

🧮 Stop Loss

The pips (smallest price movements) at which you exit a losing trade.

📈 Leverage

Multiplier for your trading power. 1x = no leverage, 2x = twice your capital. Use carefully!

Understanding Results

  • ROI: Return on Investment percentage
  • Win Rate: Actual winning percentage of simulated trades
  • Max Drawdown: Largest peak-to-trough decline during trading
  • Profit Factor: Gross profit / Gross loss ratio (higher is better)

Tips for Success

  • Start conservative and gradually increase risk as you gain experience
  • Always use stop losses to protect your capital
  • A win rate of 50%+ with good risk/reward is sustainable
  • Track your actual trades against these projections
  • Drawdown is inevitable - plan for it in your capital allocation
